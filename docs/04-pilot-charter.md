# Public Civic Dialogue Pilot — Charter

## Purpose
A neutral, non-binding consultation about democratic participation, institutional trust and safeguards for digital public services.

## Participation
The open consultation is self-selected. Its results describe participants only and must never be presented as the will of all residents or voters. A representative survey requires a separate scientific sampling design and independent research partner.

## Neutrality
No candidate or party is endorsed. Candidate-specific questions, if any, must be balanced, optional and accompanied by equivalent information. Funding, operators, methodology, dates and limitations are published.

## Data protection
Use no electoral register, identity documents or wallet credentials. Collect the minimum information necessary. Avoid special-category personal data. Publish retention and deletion rules, privacy notice and a contact point. Provide non-digital access.

## Reporting
Publish questionnaire, methodology, participation counts, limitations, aggregate results, code version and independent evaluation.
