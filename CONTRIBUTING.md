# Contributing

We welcome non-partisan contributions from civic technologists, constitutional lawyers, election researchers, accessibility specialists, privacy engineers and civil society.

1. Open an issue before substantial work.
2. Document jurisdiction, assumptions, risks and alternatives.
3. Never submit personal data, credentials, voter rolls, private keys or real ballots.
4. Security-critical changes require independent review.
5. Do not invent cryptographic primitives or claim certification.
6. Keep demos clearly marked as non-binding and non-production.
7. Disclose financial, political and institutional conflicts of interest.

A contribution does not imply endorsement by the United Nations, a government, an election authority or any political party.
