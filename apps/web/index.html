WODA
DEMOCRACY OS

Open infrastructure for inclusive civic participation, privacy-preserving public services and independently verifiable democratic processes.

Important: This is an independent research and civic-dialogue initiative, not an official election platform. It does not collect votes, verify voter eligibility or represent the United Nations. Never enter personal data, wallet credentials or real ballots.

Build trust into democratic technology

Human rights first

Participation must respect dignity, equality, privacy, political pluralism and each community's right to shape its own institutions.

Federated by design

Local and national institutions retain authority. Open standards enable cooperation without a global voter register or central ballot box.

Verifiable and inclusive

Independent scrutiny, accessibility, transparent methodology and non-digital participation are essential safeguards.

Germany as a first case study

We explore constitutional pathways, including Article 54 and Article 79 of the German Basic Law, without presuming that direct election or internet voting is already lawful or desirable. Any reform belongs to Germany's constitutional institutions and people.
